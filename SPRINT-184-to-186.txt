YOU FOUND A REAL BUG, AND IT WAS ON A FIFTH OF THE SITE
The "Cubicle Golfer Tip" box: right width, text stopping short of the edge.
That exact defect rendered 57 times across 55 pages.

THE CAUSE — a gap between two rules, neither of which is wrong
global.css governs width in two places:
  Rule A caps p/ul/ol inside anything NOT on an exclusion list.
  Rule B caps the BOX itself for every listed note class.
A <div> with inline styles and NO class is on neither list. So its paragraph
got capped at --prose-w (722px at a 1440px window) while the box ran the full
shell (1304px) — 582px of empty colour inside a coloured box. No existing
check could see it because each rule, examined alone, is correct.

FIXED: a new .art-note class that appears on BOTH lists, so box and text always
agree. Two of the 17 source instances live in the article template, which is
why they multiplied to 57. 57 -> 0 site-wide.
GUARDED: scripts/validate-box-width.ts, ceiling 0. PROVEN FAILING — reverting
the template tip box to a classless div trips it at 6.

YOUR OTHER TWO ITEMS ARE NOT A BUG, AND I HAVE NOT CHANGED THEM
The QUICK ANSWER box and the "If you're still pacing off yardages" intro are
narrower than the bars above and below them ON PURPOSE. A sprint before this
one made that call deliberately and wrote down why, including the sentence:
"There will always be space to the right of those, and that is the measure
doing its job, not a bug."

The measured trade-off, at a 1440px window:
     full shell width ........ 1304px  -> 133 characters per line
     prose measure ............ 722px  ->  74 characters per line
     WCAG 1.4.8 readability ceiling ..  80 characters per line
So the gap you are seeing is 582px, and closing it by widening the text would
put every paragraph 66% past the readability ceiling. The site's typography
being better than its competitors' is one of the few things the design audit
found it already doing right.

I am NOT going to flip that on my own judgement — it reverses a documented,
measured decision and changes all 215 article pages. Three options, your call:

  A  LEAVE IT. Prose boxes stay at the measure. Ragged right edge persists.
  B  BOXES FULL WIDTH, TEXT STILL CAPPED. One clean edge — but then every
     quick-answer box looks like the tip box you just reported as wrong:
     582px of empty colour. I would not recommend this one.
  C  NARROW THE WHOLE COLUMN to about 880px so text fills every box at ~78
     characters per line, and let tables, product grids and the comparison
     table break out wider than the column. This is what quality editorial
     sites do, it gives you ONE clean edge, and it keeps readability.
     This is my recommendation, and it is the biggest change of the three.

Say A, B or C and I will do it as the next sprint.
