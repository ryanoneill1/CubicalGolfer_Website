# Title & meta description rewrites — 16 underperforming pages

_These 16 pages held 84,977 impressions but only 1,199 clicks (1.41% CTR) over the last 3 months. Titles lead with the exact head term, stay under 60 characters, and use verifiable specifics (counts, prices, year) instead of testing claims the body cannot support. Descriptions answer the query in the first clause, cite a concrete detail, and end with a reason to click. Only `title` and `description` were changed in `articles.ts`; two pages render from standalone `.astro` files (noted) and the compression chart's H1 was updated per Task 4._
