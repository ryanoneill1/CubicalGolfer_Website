### Honest read on the "zero rich-result clicks" problem

The Product schema is already ~95% star-eligible (effective coverage above), so **missing `reviewRating` is not why rich results earn zero clicks.** Google renders review/product rich results at its discretion and withholds them from lower-authority domains regardless of correct markup. The lever is domain authority (backlinks), not more ratings. Adding ratings to the remaining sections is still worth doing for completeness, but it will not by itself unlock rich-result clicks.
