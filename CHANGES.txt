WHAT I FOUND, AND THE ONE THING YOU SHOULD CHECK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
You said to use the best information I had, or a similar ball. I did not have
to estimate — but the answer is not quite the ball the query names, and that
is worth knowing.

THERE ARE TWO BRIDGESTONE e9s.

  e9 (e-series)      The old one. Long discontinued. Bridgestone published no
                     compression figure for it and no independent measurement
                     of that exact ball exists.

  e9 Long Drive      Launched 2023, still sold. Two-piece, seamless Surlyn
                     cover, 330-dimple pattern, gradational core.
                     BRIDGESTONE PUBLISHES ITS COMPRESSION: 75.

I charted the e9 Long Drive at 75, attributed to Bridgestone (published) —
the same bar every other sourced ball on the chart clears. No estimate, no
borrowed figure from a similar ball.

The note on the entry says all of this in plain words, so a reader searching
"bridgestone e9 compression" lands on the current ball, gets a real sourced
number, and is told the older e9 is a different ball with no published figure.
That is more honest than silently charting one as the other.

If you know the query is really about the old e-series ball, tell me and I
will reword — but I would not invent a number for it, and borrowing the e12's
would be guessing.
