2. THE BUILD MADE ME REMOVE THE UNDER-$200 COMPARISON TABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

    /best-golf-gps-watch-under-200/ (ceiling $200)

3. THREE OTHER ERRORS FOUND WHILE DOING IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) A MIS-KEYED TABLE ROW — readers sent to the wrong watch.
   /best-gps-golf-watch-high-handicappers/ had a row labelled "Garmin
   Approach S12", priced and spec'd as the S12 (30 hrs battery), that
   linked to affiliateKey garmin-approach-s42 — a $299 watch. Anyone
   clicking the S12 landed on the S42. Key corrected to the S12.

   I found this by making a mistake: I "fixed" that row's $299 to $199 to
   match the S12 name, and validate-table-vs-registry failed because $199
   did not match the S42 it actually linked. The validator was right and I
   was wrong, which is how the real bug surfaced.

b) THE TABLE OF CONTENTS PROMISED TWO WATCHES THAT DO NOT EXIST.
   /best-golf-gps-watch-under-200/ listed "Best Battery Life: Garmin
   Approach S42" and "Budget Pick: Bushnell Ion Edge" — no such sections,
   and the S42 is $299 so it could never belong on this page, while the Ion
   Edge is not in your registry at all. The TOC also listed a "GPS Watch vs
   Rangefinder" section that does not exist and omitted the two that do.
   TOC now matches the four real sections.

c) SELF-CONTRADICTING AND FALSE CLAIMS, now fixed:
   - intro said "For $50 more, the Bushnell Ion Elite ($199)" — $50 more
     than $199, priced at $199, in one sentence
   - intro said "the only 4 models we recommend under $200" on a 2-product
     page
   - comparison intro ended mid-sentence: "the two best GPS golf watches
     under $200 in."
   - two pros bullets claimed "cheapest reliable golf GPS watch available"
     and "lowest price with all core GPS features". At $220 both are false
     on their own pages — the Shot Scope V5 ($210) and the Garmin S12
     ($199) undercut it. Rewritten without the superlative.
   - an FAQ said accuracy "is the same at $149 and $499" where $149 was a
     stale iON Elite price; now phrased as a price tier, not a product.
