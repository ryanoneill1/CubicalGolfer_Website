THE PART THAT MATTERED MORE: THE ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Noodle at 69 is not a slow-swing ball, and the chart's softest ball is no
longer the Supersoft. That broke 79 statements across 13 pages — 30 of them
recommendations, 13 on /best-golf-balls-for-seniors/ alone.

47 were plain figures and were renumbered. 10 could not be renumbered because
the sentence itself was now false, and those were rewritten:

  · "At 34 compression the Noodle is the softest ball we track... the pick
    for a beginner with a genuinely slow swing" — now points at the Wilson
    DUO Soft+ (40) and Callaway Supersoft (44), and says plainly that Ball
    Lab gauges the Noodle at double TaylorMade's claim.
  · "65 to 75 mph: Supersoft or TaylorMade Noodle (34)" — now Supersoft or
    DUO Soft+.
  · "The Noodle (34), Supersoft and DUO Soft+ all sit in that band" — the
    Noodle no longer does; replaced with the Soft Response (50).
  · a link whose anchor text read "TaylorMade Noodle at 34 compression"
  · the TaylorMade range sentence listed the Noodle first as the soft end;
    at 69 it sits between the Soft Response and the Tour Response.

The softest three on the chart are now Wilson DUO Soft+ 40, Callaway
Supersoft 44, TaylorMade Soft Response 50.
