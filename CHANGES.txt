--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS IS FOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Search Console, 28 days to 2 November: 109 queries ask for the compression of
ONE named ball — "titleist tour soft compression", "callaway supersoft
compression rating", "tp5 compression". 3,053 impressions, 23 clicks, average
position 8-11.

We had the answer to every one of them in balls.ts and nowhere to put it. A
reader asking for a single number landed on a 36-row table.

Two things I checked before building, because both would have killed it:

  NOT A DATA GAP.    18 of the 19 highest-demand balls were already in
                     balls.ts. Only the Bridgestone e9 is missing.
  NOT AN AUTHORITY WALL. On "titleist tour soft compression" and "callaway
                     supersoft compression rating" we are not on page 1 —
                     while ballcaddie, foundgolfballs, mycustomgolfball,
                     golfinsideruk, outofboundsgolf and shottalk all are.
                     Sites our size are winning these.

What was missing was a destination. That is what this builds.

Worth having right: compression queries at position 1-3 convert at 10.54%,
the best rate on the site — measured WITH AI Overviews present on the SERP.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT SHIPPED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
NEW  src/pages/golf-ball-compression/[ball].astro
     One page per live ball (34), at /golf-ball-compression/<affKey>/.
     EVERY FIGURE IS DERIVED FROM balls.ts — compression, cover, price, speed
     range, source, notes, and the softer/firmer neighbours. Nothing is typed
     by hand, so these cannot drift from the chart, the PDF or the ball finder
     the way the finder had drifted before Sprint 214.

     Each page: the number in the first sentence, a spec table, where it sits
     among the 34, provenance where balls.ts records a source, the editorial
     note, one affiliate CTA, the rest of that brand's range cross-linked, and
     four FAQs built from the same data.

NEW  src/pages/sitemap-ball-compression.xml.ts
     Generated from the same array, registered in the sitemap index. Add a ball
     to balls.ts and its page, its chart row and its sitemap entry all appear
     together — there is no list here to forget.

     Dates inherit from the compression chart record rather than claiming a
     freshness of their own, because these pages restate figures it owns.

MOD  src/pages/golf-ball-compression-chart/index.astro
     Ball names in both tables now link to their page — 34 inbound links from
     the site's highest-traffic page. Discontinued balls stay unlinked.

MOD  scripts/update-lastmod.ts
     Registers the 34 pages. The file walk deliberately skips dynamic routes,
     so without this they would have been invisible to the manifest and the
     sitemap would have gone stale silently. Hash combines the ball record AND
     the route file, so editing either marks the page changed.

MOD  scripts/generate-llms-full.ts
     Same reason — the static walk skips dynamic routes, so the coverage
     validator failed at 82% until these were added.

MOD  src/pages/sitemap.xml.ts     one new child sitemap in the index
MOD  src/data/lastmod-manifest.json   305 -> 339 entries

  · affiliate CTA rendered before the FTC disclosure on all 34 — added
    EarlyDisclosure above it

    fixed with a 32px rule in a stylesheet, not an inline style, so it adds
    nothing to the inline-style ceiling
  · numeric columns were right-aligned under left-aligned headers
  · llms-full.txt coverage fell to 82%

And two the build could NOT catch, which I found by reading the rendered page:
  · "a ultra-soft" on every sub-50 ball — the article now follows the word
  · the spec table read "0-80 mph" where the prose said "up to 80 mph"
