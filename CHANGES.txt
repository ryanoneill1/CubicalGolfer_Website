A CLAIM THAT REVERSED — AND WHY THAT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Yesterday the Supersoft moved 38 -> 44 and I re-pointed five pages of
beginner and senior advice to the Wilson DUO Soft+ at 40 as the softest ball
on the chart.

Today the DUO Soft+ measured 46. The Supersoft is the softest ball again, and
every one of those sentences had to be turned back.

That is my error, not new information. Had I researched all the figures
before rewriting any prose, the advice would have been re-pointed once
instead of twice. If there is a third pass, the figures all land before a
word of copy changes.

Softest four now: Supersoft 44, DUO Soft+ 46, Soft Response 50, Amazon
Basics Core Soft 55.
