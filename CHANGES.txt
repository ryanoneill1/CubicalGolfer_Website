WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The registry carried the Blue Tees Rainmaker at ~$599, verified 16 October.
PlayBetter sells it at $499.99 today, and Blue Tees list the same on their
own site. Checked twice on 1 November, hours apart, so it is a real price
drop and not a flash sale.

That is a $100 overstatement on a live product recommendation, in the
direction that makes you look expensive to anyone who clicks through.

It also broke a page. /blue-tees-rainmaker-vs-swing-caddie-sc4-pro/ was
titled "Same $599, Different Buy" and its whole argument was that the two
units cost the same so the app fee decides it. That premise is now false.
