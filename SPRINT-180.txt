SPRINT 180 — homepage spacing: the two things Ryan reported, and why they happened
==================================================================================
7 files. Upload preserving folder structure. No deletions required.

THE TWO REPORTS
  1. "NOT SURE WHERE TO START? and ⭐ FEATURED PICK are too close together"
     Correct, and worse than described. They were not close — they were TOUCHING,
     and the featured box also ran edge-to-edge past the card grid above it.
       - the intent <section> had `padding:32px clamp(1.5rem,5vw,4rem) 0`
         → a ZERO bottom padding
       - the featured block was a bare <div> with `margin:0 0 28px`
         → a ZERO top margin, AND no container, so no max-width and no side padding
     Total gap between them: 0px. Now 40px, and both share one 1200px container.

  2. "this section seems smooshed" (disclosure → buttons → byline → POPULAR)
     Correct, and the cause was structural, not cosmetic. The byline was a CHILD
     of .hero-btns, which is `display:flex; gap:12px; flex-wrap:wrap`. Ryan's
     photo and name were being laid out as a third button. It is a sibling now.
     The disclosure also had `margin-bottom:0`, so it sat flush on the gold
     button; it has 28px now.

WHY NEITHER WAS CAUGHT
  Every one of those values was an inline style="". ~50 validators run on this
  build and not one of them can see inside an inline style. This is the F9
  inline-style problem showing up as something Ryan could see.
