── TWO BUGS IN MY OWN SCRIPT, BOTH CAUGHT BEFORE ANY WRITE ───────────────────
The inserter writes once at the end, so both failures left articles.ts
byte-identical. I verified that with cmp after the first one rather than
assuming it.

  1. I anchored on "\n    slug: '...'". That is not universal — some records
     put id and slug on one line: "id: 'bf-golf-deals', slug: '/black-friday-
     golf-deals/',". And the same string appears inside every related[] entry.
     Fixed by scanning all occurrences and taking the one not followed by
     "label:".
  2. I then anchored the insert on quickAnswerProduct being near the slug. On
     /how-golf-launch-monitors-work/ it sits more than 2000 characters away.
     Fixed by inserting after the slug line instead — field order in an object
     literal is irrelevant, so the extra anchor bought nothing.
