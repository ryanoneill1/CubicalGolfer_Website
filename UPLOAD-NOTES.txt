  images and full-width blocks only - it does not stretch reading lines.
  --content-w is one variable used in 7 rules in global.css and 0 components,
  so this is a single point of change.
  The nav is full-width with its own padding, so nothing goes out of
  alignment. The @media (max-width:1200px) rule only governs the hero grid.
