── THE NEW GUARD ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
scripts/validate-placeholder-images.ts, wired into npm run validate.

It measures what share of an image is a single flat colour:

    placeholder cards   84-88% one colour
    real photography     8-19% one colour

The 70% threshold sits in the middle of a gap that wide, so there is nothing
to tune. It only counts a flat DARK GREEN field, which is what stops it firing
on real product shots taken on black or white seamless - my first version did
exactly that and flagged the TaylorMade SIM Max driver photo, so the rule is
tighter now.

It checks the 333 images a reader can actually see (sectionImage, plus the
photo of any product placed on a page). Unused placeholders sitting in public/
are listed as a note, not a failure - dead weight is not a bug.

I tested it by putting the rangefinder placeholder back on its page. It failed
the build and named the page. Then I removed it again and it passed.
