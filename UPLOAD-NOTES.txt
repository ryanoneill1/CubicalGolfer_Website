== WHAT CHANGED ==============================================================
127 headings across 59 pages. Headings only — no body text, products, prices,
links or claims.

    Best for Seniors: Bushnell Tour V7 Shift
 -> Best Rangefinder for Seniors: Bushnell Tour V7 Shift

    Most Forgiving: Ping G430 Max ($419)
 -> Most Forgiving Driver: Ping G430 Max ($419)

    Best Under $25: TaylorMade Noodle
 -> Best Golf Ball Under $25: TaylorMade Noodle

    Best Overall Under $700: Square Golf
 -> Best Overall Launch Monitor Under $700: Square Golf

Four prefix shapes, each with its own rule so the noun lands in the right place
rather than being bolted on the end:

    "Best for X"          -> Best {noun} for X
    "Best Overall Under $N" -> Best Overall {noun} Under $N
    "Best [Value] Under $N" -> Best {noun} Under $N
    short adjective phrase  -> {phrase} {noun}

Full list of all 127 in CHANGES.txt, with the 31 skips and their reasons.
