── WHY ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Two pages hold ~79,000 impressions — 12% of the whole site — at under 1% CTR.
Both rank page one (positions 8.2 and 9.1). I first called this a title-length
problem. It is not. I read both live SERPs and found two different causes.

  /average-golf-handicap/
      An AI Overview opens the SERP and states "14.2 for men and 27.5 for
      women" — the exact figures in our own meta description. Google answers
      the query above the fold and credits Scoring Zone, who also holds rank 1
      with a title that is almost ours:
          Scoring Zone   Average Golf Handicap by Age & Gender (2026 Real Data)
          Us             Average Golf Handicap by Age & Gender (2026)
      We were competing with a near-copy of our own title, from below, under an
      answer box that had already given away the number.

  /golf-club-distance-chart/
      An image pack sits above every web result (Club Up Golf, Instagram, Golf
      Sidekick — not us), plus an AI Overview printing the whole table. But
      "People also search for" lists *golf club distance chart pdf* and
      *printable golf club distance chart*, and a PINTEREST PIN ranks page one
      for "Mens Golf Club Distance Chart Pdf". GSC agrees independently:
      "golf club distance chart pdf" and "senior golf distance chart pdf" both
      earn impressions.

      We have had public/downloads/golf-club-distance-chart.pdf for months.
      The title and meta never mentioned it.

── RISK I AM NOT HIDING ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The distance-chart title drops the literal words "Carry by Swing Speed", which
currently rank. The meta still carries "carry", "total", "driver swing speeds"
and "75-115 mph", and the H1 still reads "Enter Your Swing Speed", so the terms
remain on the page — but the exact-phrase title match goes. The compression
page ranks #1 without "swing speed" in its title, which is why I think the
trade is right. It is still a trade, and it is reversible in one line.

Changing a title that already ranks on page one can move rankings either way.
Give it 14 days before judging, and judge on CTR in GSC, not on position.
