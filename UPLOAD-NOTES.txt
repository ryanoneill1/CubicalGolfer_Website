== WHAT CHANGED ===============================================================
110 section headings across 60 pages. Headings only. No body text, no products,
no prices, no links, no claims added or removed.

The pattern, everywhere:

    Best Overall: Titleist Pro V1
 -> Best Golf Ball Overall: Titleist Pro V1

    Best Premium: ECCO Biom C4
 -> Best Premium Golf Shoe for Walking: ECCO Biom C4

    Best Overall 2026: Garmin Approach S44
 -> Best Golf GPS Watch Overall: Garmin Approach S44

Only headings whose prefix carried NO topic noun were touched — "Best Overall",
"Best Budget", "Best Value", "Best Premium", "Best Mid-Range", "Softest Feel"
and similar. Headings that were already specific ("Best Carry Bag", "Best for
Putting") were left exactly as they were.

Full list of all 110 in CHANGES.txt.


== WHY ========================================================================
Comparing /best-golf-balls-for-seniors/ against independentgolfreviews.com, who
outranks us, the one difference I could act on was heading shape: theirs read
like searches ("Best Low Compression Golf Balls for Seniors"), ours read like a
product list ("Best Value: Wilson DUO Soft+").

It also matches the strongest pattern in your own data — qualified targets reach
page 1 at 32%, generic ones at 3%.

I shipped that to one page in Sprint 201 and then suggested waiting three weeks
to see if it worked. That was the wrong call: it protected a tidy experiment at
the cost of your traffic. Shipping it across all 60 pages gets the same
attribution — the 60 touched pages against everything untouched — and gets it
now instead of in November.

== TWO THINGS I BROKE AND FIXED ===============================================
1. APOSTROPHE. My topic map contained "Men's Golf Glove". Written into a
   single-quoted TypeScript string that produced:

       h2: 'Best Premium Men's Golf Glove: Titleist Players Flex'

   — a syntax error that would have failed the build. Worse, my script wrote the
   file BEFORE parsing it, so CURRENT was left broken.

   Fixed three ways: restored CURRENT from the Sprint 201 build and re-verified
   190/192 hashes; changed the topic to "Golf Glove for Men"; and added a guard
   that rejects any new heading containing a quote or backslash.

   The real fix is the third one: the script now PARSE-CHECKS THE WHOLE FILE
   BEFORE WRITING ANYTHING. If the parse fails, nothing is written at all.

2. WORD STUTTER. The forgiving-drivers page produced "Most Forgiving Forgiving
   Driver Overall". Added a collapse step for repeated adjacent words and
   re-scanned all 110 — zero stutters remain.
