── THE FINDING (Batch 4) ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
/golf-ball-complete-guide/ carries **86 inbound internal links** — more than any
other page on the site. It is 940 rendered words and it had exactly **one**
affiliate link.

Its Quick Answer reads:

    Best overall value: Callaway Supersoft ($25/dz) for most weekend golfers.
    Best premium: Titleist Pro V1 ($55/dz) for 95+ mph swingers.
    [ Check Supersoft Price at Amazon → ]

A reader who works out they are a 95+ mph swinger and wants the Pro V1 has
nowhere to go. And it is worse further down — "Best Ball by Handicap Level"
names eight balls across four handicap bands (Supersoft, Srixon Soft Feel,
Chrome Soft, Titleist AVX, Kirkland Signature, Pro V1, Pro V1x, Bridgestone
e12). One of the eight is buyable.

/golf-simulator-complete-guide/ is the same shape: names the Garmin R10 ($490)
and the SkyTrak+ ($1,195), sells the R10 only.

This is the first MONETISATION finding in five batches. Everything before it was
content quality. The site's internal link equity is concentrated on a page that
cannot convert.


── THE FIX ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Both pages get <RecommendedGearGrid />, the component /launch-monitor-buying-
guide/ already uses. Nothing new was invented — the component, the prop name
(`items`) and the item shape ({ key, role, name }) are copied verbatim from that
page's working GUIDE_GEAR usage.

  golf-ball-complete-guide — four balls mapped to the handicap bands the page
  has just finished describing:
      callaway-supersoft   High handicap and seniors — low compression
      srixon-soft-feel     Budget pick — soft feel, ionomer cover
      kirkland-signature   Mid handicap — urethane without the premium
      titleist-pro-v1      Low handicap — premium urethane, 90 to 105 mph

  golf-simulator-complete-guide — the two it already names:
      garmin-approach-r10  Entry setup — radar, works in tight spaces
      skytrak-plus         Simulator-grade — photometric accuracy

Placed immediately before the "All Our ... Content" hub section, so it sits
right after the recommendations and before the onward links. The reader is told
which one suits them, then given the button, then offered the deeper guides.

All six product keys verified present in affiliate-links.ts before use.

No prices in any role string — deliberate, same rule as Sprints 193 and 194, so
validate-prose-prices / validate-section-body-prices / validate-same-page-prices
have nothing new to check.

Inline styles: 3 -> 3 and 2 -> 2. The grid brings its own styling, so the
ratchet is untouched.


── THE ONE THING I COULD NOT VERIFY ──────────────────────────────────────────
I do not hold src/components/RecommendedGearGrid.astro — no sprint has shipped
it to me. So I could not read the component to confirm its prop contract.

What I did instead: copied the exact working usage from /launch-monitor-buying-
guide/, which renders 12 affiliate links through this component on the live site
today. Same prop name, same item shape, same key format. If the component takes
anything else optionally, these two pages simply will not use it.

If the build fails on this, it will fail loudly at the component boundary and
