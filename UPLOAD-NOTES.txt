Sprint 128 — Fix the compression chart table overflow (my bug from 124a)

EXTRACT AT THE REPOSITORY ROOT.

THE BUG
Sprint 124a added a 7th column ("Source") to the compression chart table.
That pushed the BUY column off the right edge - the money column on the page
that carries 35% of the site's clicks was invisible without horizontal
scrolling. I added the column without checking the rendered width. My error.

THE FIX
  - Source column removed. The source now sits UNDER the compression number
    in the same cell, small and muted. Same information, same checkability,
    one less column. Back to 6 columns.
  - Per-ball note text reduced from 16px to 14px. It was set at full body
    size, which is why rows ran eight lines deep and squeezed everything else.
  - global.css: the ball column now takes the slack (38%, min 260px) and the
    Buy cell is pinned nowrap/right so it cannot be compressed again.

VERIFIED in built HTML: 6 columns, 34 rows, Buy cell renders "Check price ->"
in full, all 17 sourced rows still show their source, sorting untouched (it
reads data-* attributes on the row, not column indices).

3 files, all modifications. No deletions.

Worth a look on your phone once deployed - the table still scrolls
horizontally on narrow screens by design, but the Buy column should now be
reachable much sooner.
