── WHY LAST SPRINT DID NOT MOVE THEM ─────────────────────────────────────────
You asked twice and I did not do it. The reason was not stubbornness and it
was not a styling problem.

Those four sections were sitting OUTSIDE </BaseLayout>, appended at the very
bottom of the .astro file AFTER the <style> block:

    </style>
    <section>
      <h2 id="by-need">Skip the Chart ...
      <h2 id="brand-summary">Compression Chart by Brand ...
      <h2 id="low-to-high">All 35 Balls Ranked ...
      <h2 id="temperature">Does Temperature Affect ...
    </section>

Astro renders stray markup like that by appending it to the body, which is
exactly why it always landed below the related guides AND below the embed
box, no matter what. Dangling markup, not a layout decision.

In Sprint 137 I restyled those blocks in place and reported them done. I
never checked WHERE they sat in the document, so "move them up" appeared to
do nothing — because I had not moved anything. That is on me, and it is the
second time this week I have confirmed a change from the source instead of
from the rendered order.

── THE FIX ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
All four sections moved INSIDE the article, immediately after the FAQ block
and before "More Improve Your Golf Game Guides".

Rendered order now, verified by byte offset in the BUILT HTML:

    Frequently Asked Questions
    Skip the Chart — Pick by What You Need
    Compression Chart by Brand — Quick Summary
    All 35 Balls Ranked Low to High Compression
    Does Temperature Affect Golf Ball Compression?
    More Improve Your Golf Game Guides
    </article>
    embed box

The temperature section came along because it was in the same orphaned
block. Leaving it behind would have stranded it alone below the related
guides, which would have looked worse than before.

── NOTHING LOST ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
All 35 ball names still present in the rendered page, checked against
balls.ts. Six brand cards, four band blocks, no section duplicated. They are
now inside <article>, so they inherit the article's spacing and width instead
of floating free.
