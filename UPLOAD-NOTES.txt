── A BIGGER ONE I FOUND WHILE IN THERE ───────────────────────────────────────
This page was telling Google two different last-modified dates:

  sitemap lastmod        2026-09-29   (computed from a content hash)
  on-page schema         2026-07-23   (hand-held in articles.ts)

It is not just this page. I measured it properly with a real parse, not a
regex: 149 of 190 articles show an on-page date OLDER than their own sitemap
entry. Some by five months. /best-putters-yips/ prints "Updated 2026-04-20 —
prices and availability checked monthly" when we actually touched it 1 Sept.
That reads as a false claim to a reader, and it was only ever a stale stamp.

src/lib/freshness.ts takes the LATER of the two dates. Applied to the
compression chart ONLY in this sprint. Both schema blocks and the visible stamp
now agree.

I have deliberately NOT rolled it out to the other 148. It changes a visible
date on 149 pages and that deserves its own sprint and its own measurement
window. Say the word and it is a small change — the helper already exists.
