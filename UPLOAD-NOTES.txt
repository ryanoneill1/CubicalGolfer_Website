── TWO THINGS I FOUND WHILE IN THERE ─────────────────────────────────────────

A. The embed was shipping our OLD, WRONG numbers to other people's sites.
   public/embeds/compression-chart.html is the copy-paste block we offer for
   backlinks. It was hand-written and never updated, so it still had Titleist
   TruFeel at 45 (correct: 70) and Vice Drive at 55 (correct: 95) — the
   PRE-124a figures. Anyone who embedded it was republishing our worst data
   under our name, linking back to a page that disagreed.

   It is now generated from balls.ts by scripts/generate-embed-chart.ts, wired
   into the build. It cannot drift again, because it is no longer a separate
   copy of the data. "Best For" is now the swing-speed window from the data
   instead of free text, for the same reason.

B. Every Golf Galaxy click on the compression chart was being logged to
   Plausible as an Amazon click.
   data-outbound="amazon" was hardcoded on all 62 outbound links, on the main
   chart and the five brand charts. Three balls there go to Golf Galaxy
   (Soft Response, Maxfli Tour X, and now Tour S). AffiliateCTA derives this
   correctly; these two files never did.

   The chart is ~35% of site clicks, so this has been quietly inflating the
   Amazon EPC the whole model rests on. Now derived from the destination URL.
   Built HTML confirms 58 amazon / 4 golfgalaxy where it was 62 / 0.

   Worth knowing: your $0.1625 Amazon EPC is measured slightly high. I can't
   say how much until a week of clean data comes in.
